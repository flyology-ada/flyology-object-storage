## MODEL: the pinned botocore model, the generator, the descriptor and the registry tools (no findings)

The area covers the pinned model `review/baseline/service-2.json`, the generator `tools/generate-s3-model.py`, the committed descriptor `src/flyology-object_storage-s3-model.ads` and its 78,882-line generated body `-s3-model.adb`, the registry and codegen tools `tools/s3_operation.py`, `tools/s3-operation.py`, `tools/s3_codegen.py`, `tools/verify-botocore-model.sh` and `tools/verify-coverage.sh`, the ledger files under `coverage/`, the five committed generated XML serializers `src/flyology-object_storage-s3-generated_*.adb`, and every descriptor consumer in `src/` — `client-low_level.adb`, `s3-listings.adb`, `s3-versions.adb`, `s3-multipart.adb`, `s3-multipart_uploads.adb` and `server-s3_applications.adb` — read against `docs/architecture/s3-operation-automation.md` and the CI wiring in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`; the discovery report found no actionable finding and the verification upheld that.

The verification did not re-run the generator, on the ground that regenerating and diffing (what `generate --check` and the discovery both did) proves only that the generator is a function of its input and cannot detect a rule the generator itself gets wrong. It instead ran a generator-independent differential, `tests/repros/python/model_differential.py` — python3 standard library only, no Alire build, nothing tracked touched — which parses the committed Ada text directly with a line-exact parser that raises on any unrecognised line, re-derives every expected value from the pinned JSON with the REST-XML rules written out in its own source, and compares the two. It exits **0** (0 = no mismatch, 1 = at least one mismatch, 2 = missing input), runs in about a second and is byte-for-byte deterministic across repeated runs. It compared 116 operations, 718 shapes, 1,699 structure members, 279 enumeration values in 75 shapes and 38 error-shape links across 20 distinct error shapes, over all 41 accessors declared in the `.ads` (29 flat, 12 indexed) and 30,303 value arms plus 3,607 `when others` defaults — 33,910 of the 33,910 `return` statements in the body, an identity the program asserts so that "no mismatch" cannot mean "did not look" — together with 35 operations carrying `httpChecksum`, 96 using the `responseCode` 200 default, 99 `requestUri`s with a query literal and 30 with a greedy label, 8 shapes with `min`, 7 with `max`, 4 with `pattern`, 8 with `timestampFormat`, 1 with `xmlNamespace` and 35 flattened, 63 payload shapes, 7 streaming members and 6 header maps, and 7,615 emitted strings screened for non-ASCII (0 unsafe); the recomputed digest `429763d64912af5edae4c7a0f20a8ac3e6fecf734cde5fc465016bc8badcdef9` matches the `.ads` constant, `tools/generate-s3-model.py:15-19`, `tools/verify-botocore-model.sh:12` and `coverage/s3-operations.toml:5`. That the checker is not vacuous was established by a mutation control: twenty single-token mutations were applied to a copy of `src/` under the scratchpad directory — never the worktree — and all twenty were caught with exit 1 and a first-line diagnosis naming the right element, spanning a changed `Request_URI`, `Response_Code` and HTTP method, one member `locationName`, one enumeration value, the spec SHA pin and `Shape_Count`, a `Minimum`, an `Is_Flattened`, a `Timestamp_Format`, a `Request_Checksum_Required`, a deleted `Shape_Name` case arm, two swapped `Member_Name` values, a `Member_Required`, a `Member_Shape`, an `Error_Shape`, a `Location`, and three consumer member ordinals.

## Coverage evidence: MODEL

- The differential reads `review/baseline/service-2.json` with an `object_pairs_hook` that reports duplicate object keys (none exist) and preserves member insertion order, which is the order the descriptor's ordinals and every consumer literal depend on; it reads `src/flyology-object_storage-s3-model.adb` with a line-exact parser for the two emitted shapes (`when KEY => return VALUE;` and the nested `case Member/Index is`), decoding Ada string literals including `""` escapes, so a layout change cannot be silently mis-read as agreement; and it reads `-s3-model.ads` for `Model_SHA256`, `Operation_Count`, `Shape_Count`, the four enumeration types and the declaration text of every visible function.
- Per operation it compares `Operation_Name`, `Method`, `Request_URI`, `Response_Code` (with the 200 default re-derived rather than copied), `Input_Shape`, `Output_Shape`, `Error_Count`, the whole `Error_Shape` list, `Unsigned_Payload`, `Authentication_Type`, `Request_Checksum_Required`, `Request_Checksum_Algorithm_Member`, `Request_Validation_Mode_Member` and `Response_Checksum_Algorithms` (including that no algorithm name contains the `;` join separator, which would make the image ambiguous); independently of the descriptor it checks that every `{label}` in a `requestUri` corresponds to exactly one `uri` member whose `locationName` is that label, that no label repeats, and that every `httpChecksum` member name is an actual member of the input shape.
- Per shape it compares `Shape_Name`, `Kind`, `Location_Name`, `Payload_Member`, `Timestamp_Format`, `Pattern`, `Minimum`, `Maximum`, `Is_Flattened`, `Is_Sensitive`, `XML_Namespace`, `List_Member_Shape`, `Map_Key_Shape`, `Map_Value_Shape`, `Enumeration_Count`, the whole `Enumeration_Value` list and `Member_Count`, plus the model-side invariants that a `payload` names a member with no `location`, that `required` names existing members, that a list has a `member` and a map has `key` and `value`, that no enum list contains a duplicate, and that members appear only on structures.
- Per member it compares `Member_Name`, `Member_Shape`, `Location` (with the `body` default re-derived), `Member_Location_Name` (with the REST-XML "member name when absent" default re-derived), `Member_Required`, `Member_Flattened`, `Member_Streaming`, `Member_XML_Attribute`, `Member_XML_Namespace` and `Member_Context_Parameter`.
- Structurally it requires every flat function to have exactly one arm per operation or per shape 1..718 with no extras, every indexed function to have no arm outside its domain, the emitted function set to equal the declared set, every emitted string to be printable ASCII, and every indexed accessor to carry a `Pre` bounding the index — which is what makes the `when others` defaults unreachable under the declared contract, notably `Member_Shape => Shape_Index'First`, which would otherwise silently answer shape #1; the twelve indexed accessors were checked against their matching count accessors at `.ads:397-400`, `:499-501` and `:511-576`, and SPARK proves the callers. Any JSON attribute the checker does not classify is reported; the only one is `synthetic`, new to this run, on `AccessDenied`.
- Consumer ordinal cross-check: 74 literal member ordinals were resolved in 6 consumer files by evaluating each call site's Ada shape expression against the JSON (`Input_Shape`/`Output_Shape` of a named operation, `Member_Shape` with a literal ordinal, `List_Member_Shape`, `Map_*_Shape`, `Shape_Index` conversions, and local helper functions taking a `Positive` member index — including the three same-named nested `Valid_Enumeration` helpers in `server-s3_applications.adb`, disambiguated by nearest preceding declaration). All 74 rows are `OK`: the call-site name agrees with the member or wire name in every case, and each of the 57 ordinals that reaches an `Enumeration_Count` or `Enumeration_Value` lookup lands on a member whose shape does carry enumeration values — a load-bearing invariant, since an off-by-one onto a non-enum member would make the validator reject every non-empty value. The resolved set is a superset of the discovery's list: `PutObjectOutput` 14 (`low_level.adb:7661`) and `UploadPartOutput` 1 (`:13982`) were not enumerated there and are correct, and the discovery's `CreateMultipartUploadOutput` list (12, 14) is incomplete — 6 and 13 are also passed and are also correct.
- Descriptor omissions were accounted for exhaustively rather than spot-checked, as a summary with the consumer each would need: `operation.endpoint` (`hostPrefix`) 1, `member.hostLabel` 1, `shape.error.httpStatusCode` 38 links over 20 shapes, `shape.error`/`shape.exception` 20/20, list-item `locationName` 9, `shape.xmlNamespace.prefix` 1, `operation.input.locationName`/`.xmlNamespace` 1/1, `operation.staticContextParams` 77, `operation.deprecated`/`member.deprecated` 4/5, `shape.box`/`member.box` 43/1, `shape.union` 1, `shape.event`/`eventstream`/`member.eventpayload` 5/1/3, `member.idempotencyToken` 1, `shape.synthetic` 1. Map key and value `locationName`, member `xmlName`, `queryName`, `requiresLength`, `eventheader`, `jsonvalue` and per-member `timestampFormat` do not occur in this model at all. None of the omissions has a `src/` consumer at this commit, so none can propagate a wrong value today; each becomes a gap only if the named consumer is written against the descriptor rather than the JSON.
- The hand-typed element names in the five committed generated serializers were checked by extracting every string literal and matching it against the reachable shape closure of that operation's input and output in the pinned JSON — element names, member names, `locationName`s and enumeration values. `PutBucketAcl`, `PutBucketLogging`, `PutBucketWebsite` and `PutBucketInventoryConfiguration` have zero unmatched literals; `ListDirectoryBuckets` has only the ARN prefixes `arn:`, `s3:` and `s3express:`, which are validation text rather than element names. This refutes "a typo in an element name would not change the closure digest" for the sources as committed; it does not prove the converse, that a *missing* element would be detected, which remains gated by the signed-socket bodies and the server corpora.
- Registry and ledger consistency was re-derived independently of `tools/s3_operation.py`: `coverage/aws-s3-operations.tsv` has 117 lines whose 116 operation names are exactly `sorted(model["operations"])` in `LC_ALL=C` order, `coverage/s3-operation-counts.json` reports `operations: 116`, and `coverage/s3-operations.toml:4-5` pins botocore revision `36c34f15391da01cd717c73c0fffa747c9889768` with the same SHA-256 as the generator, the `.ads` and `tools/verify-botocore-model.sh:12`. No drift. Stated limit of this check: it establishes the internal consistency of the pin, not the pinned file's fidelity to upstream botocore at that revision. No network fetch was made, so "the pinned JSON is the right JSON" rests on the CI job that fetches commit `36c34f15` and re-runs `verify-botocore-model.sh`, not on this verification.
- The discovery independently regenerated the whole spec and body from `review/baseline/service-2.json` into the scratch directory and found them byte-identical to the committed files, with `generate --check --output-dir src` exiting 0; it also confirmed that `--check` compares the full regenerated text of both files rather than a subset, that a changed JSON cannot regenerate silently (the digest and the 116/718 counts are checked in the generator, the registry loader, the shell verifier and the registry TOML), and that a duplicated, renamed or missing operation is caught at three independent layers (`load_registry`, `verify_registry_model_inventory` and `verify-coverage.sh`, with `test-coverage-verifier.sh` as a negative oracle proving a ledger-only promotion is rejected).

## Observations not counted: MODEL

- **O-1 (dead `querystring` branch, `tools/s3_operation.py:1228-1231`):** confirmed verbatim by reading — the branch does `target = target.replace(marker, encoded)` while `encoded` is bound only in the `uri` branch at `:1219` and `:1222` — but refuted as a finding by enumerating all 116 operations, none of whose `requestUri`s contains a `{<locationName>}` for a `querystring` member, so the branch cannot execute; a latent `UnboundLocalError` in a test tool, not a product defect.
- **O-2 (diagnostic precision, `tools/s3-operation.py:237-247`, where `generate --check` without a model prints "current" after a narrower check):** outside what a JSON-versus-descriptor differential can settle and deliberately not re-litigated; nothing in the verification contradicts the discovery's disposition that CI always supplies the model and the full gate fails closed, so a stale generated Ada file cannot reach `main`.
- **O-3 (member-ordinal fragility):** upheld and now bounded — all 74 literal ordinals are correct today and the SHA pin plus the full-text `generate --check` make silent drift impossible for this model; the residual risk is a future model bump that inserts a member before one of them, which would compile and validate against the wrong shape, and `low_level.adb:11669-11673` already shows the name-based alternative (`Find_Model_Member`). `tests/repros/python/model_differential.py` is a ready regression gate for exactly that bump: standard-library-only, no build required, and its consumer table fails closed on an ordinal that moves.
- **O-4 (descriptor omissions with no consumer affected):** upheld and made exhaustive — the verification replaced the discovery's list with a complete attribute-by-attribute table with instance counts and the consumer each omission would need, and confirmed that none has a `src/` consumer at this commit.
- **O-5 (the ARN pattern on a header member, `low_level.adb:927-929`):** refuted as unreachable. All four pattern-carrying shapes were enumerated: `CopySource` and `RenameSource` are the only ones on request-serialized non-body members; `S3RegionalOrS3ExpressBucketArnString` is a header member of `CreateBucketOutput` and `HeadBucketOutput`, and `NonEmptyKmsKeyArnString` and `Bucket.BucketArn` are body members that `Prepare_Model_Request_Internal` rejects at `low_level.adb:1072-1074`. The ordinal table shows `Valid_Model_Scalar` is applied to response headers only for CreateSession (`:2735-2737`) and CopyObject (`:14725-14734`), never to HeadBucket or CreateBucket, and the residual behaviour would be a fail-closed `return False` rather than a wrong accept.
- **O-6 (XML list item-name fallback, `tools/s3_codegen.py:967-969`):** confirmed verbatim — `member.get("locationName", item.get("locationName", item_shape))` falls back to the item shape name where REST-XML uses `member` — and refuted, because the only two non-flattened lists whose member lacks `locationName`, `ObjectAttributesList` and `OptionalObjectAttributesList`, are used exclusively as header members of `GetObjectAttributesRequest`, `ListObjectVersionsRequest`, `ListObjectsRequest` and `ListObjectsV2Request`, so neither ever appears in an XML body.
- **O-7 (`Long_Long_Integer'Value` leniency, `low_level.adb:949-963` with the `'Value` call at `:955`):** confirmed present but ruled a `Valid_Model_Scalar` behaviour rather than a descriptor question, and routed to the CLIENT-LOW area, where it was escalated to CLIENT-LOW-7; the descriptor supplies the bounds correctly, comparing `Minimum` and `Maximum` as 64-bit values for both `integer` and `long` kinds, so no model-side defect contributes to it.
- **O-8 (unread checksum traits — `Request_Checksum_Required`, `Request_Validation_Mode_Member` and `Response_Checksum_Algorithms` emitted with no `src/` consumer):** outside what a JSON-versus-descriptor differential can settle and not re-litigated; it belongs to the CHECKSUM area by the discovery's own routing, which owns whether every `requestChecksumRequired` operation actually sends one.
- **O-9 (test-harness ordering, `tools/s3_operation.py:1273-1279`, where the expected request target sorts encoded `name=value` strings lexically rather than by (name, value) as SigV4 does):** not addressed by the verification, whose differential compares the descriptor with the JSON and does not reach the signed-socket harness; the discovery's disposition therefore stands unchallenged — the two orders differ only when one name is a prefix of another followed by a byte below `=`, no reviewed fixture contains such a pair, and a mismatch would fail the signed-socket test rather than ship, since the client signs with `SigV4.Canonical_Query` (`sigv4.adb:128-154`).
